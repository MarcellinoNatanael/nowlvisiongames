Careers

Work With Us

Nowl Vision Games is an indie game studio from Indonesia that develops psychological horror games rooted in local culture and folklore, from DEMIT and Perjanjian Gaib to our upcoming project, DEMIT Unbroken Chain. We are open to freelance and part-time collaborations, all fully remote. If you love horror and want to help bring these stories to life, we would like to hear from you.

More about working with us

How We Work

Remote & Flexible

Work from anywhere, as a per-project freelancer or part-time.

Local Stories, Global Players

We bring Indonesian folklore to horror players around the world.

Small Team, Real Impact

Every contributor is credited in the games we release.

Our Values

  • Honest: no false excuses and no manipulation.
  • Responsible for the work we take on.
  • Working for the team and the company, not only for ourselves.
  • Managing our time and keeping work matters separate from personal matters.
  • Respecting one another and following the rules.
